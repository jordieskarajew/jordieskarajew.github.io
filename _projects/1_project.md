This project presents my paper on whether affine-invariant Riemannian geometry on financial correlation matrices provides a more informative measure of systemic market stress than Euclidean or log-Euclidean alternatives.

The paper develops the geometric framework, explains the distinction between ambient and intrinsic distances on correlation matrices, and tests the method empirically on financial market data.
